# Domain Glossary

## Project

One private, local video-creation workspace containing source media, analysis,
editorial decisions, timeline plans, render attempts, and outputs.

## Visual Candidate

A photo or video considered for a timeline. Audio-only files are not visual
candidates.

## Duplicate Group

A conservative set of visually interchangeable candidates. It may contain
exact copies, burst photos, or a photo and video whose sampled frames show the
same moment. It never represents a face identity.

## Story Group

Candidates showing the same kind of place, activity, subject, or event. Story
groups are broader than duplicate groups: two waterfall views may belong to the
same story group without being duplicates.

## Story Beat

The editorial purpose of a selected candidate in the finished sequence:
opening, journey, highlight, detail, people, or closing. A coherent story uses
several beats and avoids repeating interchangeable moments.

## Soundtrack Asset

An owner-provided audio file that the owner is permitted to use. The default
soundtrack is the newest eligible soundtrack asset in the project. A project
may explicitly have no soundtrack.

## Pipeline Step

One derived phase of a project: import, analyze, curate, review, render, or
ready. A step is pending, current, complete, or failed. The next action is the
first actionable incomplete step.
